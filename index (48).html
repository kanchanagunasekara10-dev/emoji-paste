How to copy and paste emojis

  1. Find your emoji. Type a word in the search bar (try "heart", "fire", or "pizza"), or pick a category to browse.
  2. Click it once. A "Copied!" tooltip confirms the emoji is on your clipboard.
  3. Paste it anywhere. Press Ctrl + V on Windows, or Command + V on a Mac.

Your recent picks are saved automatically, so the emojis you use most stay one click away the next time you visit.

Where you can use these emojis

These are standard Unicode emojis, so they work almost everywhere you can type: Instagram captions and bios, TikTok comments, X posts, Facebook, Discord, Slack, WhatsApp, iMessage, Gmail, Google Docs, Microsoft Word, YouTube titles, and even file names. Because you are copying real characters rather than pictures, they keep working when someone else views your post on a different phone or computer.

Why does an emoji look different on my phone?

Each platform draws its own artwork. Apple, Google, Microsoft, and Samsung all have slightly different designs for the same emoji, so a smiley copied here may look a little different on an iPhone than on an Android phone. The meaning stays the same.

Why do some emojis show as a box or two letters?

A blank box means your device's font does not include that emoji yet, usually because it is a newer one. Country flags can also appear as two letters on Windows, since Windows does not ship flag artwork. Updating your operating system normally fixes this.

Does this work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. The page works in any modern browser on desktop, tablet, and mobile. Tap an emoji to copy it, then paste it wherever you are typing.

Do I need to install anything?
